# Tradesgiving Daily Trader

> Earn a bonus each day you make a qualifying trade from November 24 to November 30, 2026.

[Tradesgiving Daily Trader](https://docs.polymarket.us/learn/faq/tradesgiving-daily-trader.md) is a Polymarket US campaign under the **[Sequential Incentive Program](https://polymarketexchange.com/files/notices/Exchange%20Notice%20-%20Amended%20Sequential%20Incentive%20Program%20\(2026.10.08\).pdf)**. For seven days, you can earn a bonus each day you make a qualifying trade.

## The basics

Tradesgiving Daily Trader is a week-long daily bonus. Each campaign day, place one qualifying trade in any market and you earn that day's bonus. Each day stands on its own: miss a day and you can still earn the rest.

### When does it run?

| | |
| - | - |
| **First bonus day** | Tuesday, November 24, 2026, from 9:00 AM ET |
| **Last bonus day** | Monday, November 30, 2026, for trades placed before midnight ET |
| **Daily cutoff** | Trades must be placed before midnight ET (9:00 PM PT) each day |

Trades placed at or after midnight ET count toward the next bonus day. Trades placed at or after midnight ET on November 30 don't earn a bonus.

### Where do I see my offer?

Tap the **Tradesgiving Daily Trader** card in the app (Portfolio tab or the home carousel). It shows your daily bonus, your minimum trade, the days you've earned and the time left until the daily cutoff.

## Eligibility

### Do I need to opt in or sign up?

No. Eligible Participants are enrolled automatically. There's nothing to opt in to. You must have a verified Polymarket US account in good standing, in an eligible jurisdiction.

### Do I need to make a deposit?

* **Existing users:** No. Just make a qualifying trade each day.
* **New users:** Yes. Sign up and deposit \$10 once, then make a single qualifying trade of \$10 or more each day to earn \$5 for that day.

### I'm a new user. Can I take part?

Yes. New users who sign up without a promo code after 8:00 PM ET on Monday, November 23, are enrolled automatically. Deposit \$10 once. Then, on each remaining campaign day, a single qualifying trade of \$10 or more earns a bonus.

If you join partway through the week, you can earn bonuses for the days that are left. The Tradesgiving Daily Trader card shows the days that have passed and the days remaining.

New users might not be eligible for the Tradesgiving Daily Trader, but if you use a promotion or referral code during sign-up you will still receive that credit.

### I signed up with a promo code. What do I get?

If you signed up with a promo code, you'll get the deposit offer tied to that code under the [Deposit Incentive Program](https://docs.polymarket.us/incentives/user-programs.md#deposit-incentive-program). Users who sign up with a promo code aren't enrolled in the Tradesgiving Daily Trader. If you're enrolled, you'll see the Tradesgiving Daily Trader card in the app.

## Earning your daily bonus

### What counts as a qualifying trade?

A qualifying trade is **one single trade** that meets or exceeds your minimum trade amount, placed during a bonus day.

* Any market counts, in any category.
* Smaller trades are not added together. Two \$5 trades do not meet a \$10 minimum.
* Fees count toward the minimum trade amount.
* Trades at any price count.
* Buys of yes and no count.
* Selling a position / cash outs do not count. Buying the opposite side of a position you hold is a cash out and doesn't count.
* Trades must be placed before midnight ET each day. On November 24, trades count from 9:00 AM ET.

### How much can I earn, and what's my minimum trade?

Bonus amounts and trade minimums vary by Participant. Your daily bonus and minimum trade are shown on your Tradesgiving Daily Trader card. New users earn \$5 per day with a \$10 minimum trade.

### How many bonuses can I earn?

One bonus per bonus day, up to seven during the campaign. Placing more than one qualifying trade on the same day doesn't earn a second bonus.

### What if I miss a day?

That day's bonus can't be earned once the daily cutoff passes. Missing one day doesn't affect the rest of the week, so you can still earn a bonus on each remaining day.

## Getting paid

### When will my bonus arrive?

Bonuses are credited within 24 hours of a qualifying trade. You'll get a "Bonus Unlocked" message in your inbox when it's added.

### Where does my bonus go?

Bonuses are added to your Polymarket US account as promotional trading credits.

### Can I withdraw my bonus?

No. Promotional trading credits can be used to open positions and pay applicable fees, but can't be withdrawn as cash. Your own collateral is used before credits. Any balance above your credits is normal collateral that you can withdraw. See [Promotional Trading Credits](https://docs.polymarket.us/incentives/user-programs.md#promotional-trading-credits).

### Do my bonuses expire?

Promotional trading credits may expire. We recommend using your bonuses to trade soon after they're credited.

## Help

### I made a qualifying trade but didn't get my bonus.

Please allow 24 hours after your trade. Then check:

1. **Was it a single trade at or above your minimum?** Smaller trades aren't added together.
2. **Was it placed before the daily cutoff (midnight ET)?** Trades after the cutoff count toward the next day, except on November 30, the last bonus day.
3. **Has it already been paid?** Check your Tradesgiving Daily Trader card and your inbox for a "Bonus Unlocked" message.

If your bonus still hasn't arrived after 24 hours, contact Support through the app and we'll take a look.

### Can Polymarket US change or cancel Tradesgiving Daily Trader?

Polymarket US may modify, suspend or end the campaign on a prospective basis. Any changes will be posted on the Polymarket US website. Polymarket US may withhold, cancel or reverse bonuses in cases of suspected fraud, abuse, manipulation, self-dealing, coordinated activity or other activity inconsistent with the program terms or Polymarket US rules.
